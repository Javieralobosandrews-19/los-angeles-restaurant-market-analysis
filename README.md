# ☕ Los Angeles Restaurant Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the restaurant market in Los Angeles to evaluate the feasibility of launching an innovative coffee shop concept operated by robotic waiters.

The analysis explores restaurant industry trends, business characteristics, and market opportunities to support strategic decision-making for a startup considering entry into the food service sector.

---

## Business Problem

A startup plans to open a coffee shop featuring robotic waiters and needs to assess whether the concept is viable within the Los Angeles restaurant market.

The objective is to identify industry patterns, understand market conditions, and provide data-driven recommendations to support investment decisions.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze the restaurant landscape in Los Angeles.
* Identify the most common restaurant categories.
* Evaluate the prevalence of chain and independent establishments.
* Investigate seating capacity patterns.
* Explore market opportunities and potential challenges.
* Develop strategic recommendations based on data analysis.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about restaurants operating in Los Angeles, including:

* Restaurant type
* Seating capacity
* Chain affiliation
* Geographic information
* Business characteristics
